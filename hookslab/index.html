5 Proven Hook Frameworks for Short-Form Video

Short video apps love one key thing: watch time. These five simple opening lines stop the scroll right away.

⚡ 1. The Curiosity Gap
Leaves a question in the viewer mind that makes them stay for the answer.
"There is one hidden reason your videos are not getting views—and nobody talks about it."
🔥 2. The Hot Take
Shares a bold opinion to spark fast comments and discussion on your post.
"Unpopular opinion: most creators waste hours posting two times a day."
📖 3. The Storytime Hook
Starts right in the middle of a real moment when things went wrong.
"On day three of trying this routine, I was ready to quit."
🎯 4. The Direct How-To
Promises a clear, simple answer to a common problem with fast steps.
"If you are new to video editing, do this before touching any software."
🛑 5. The Pattern Interrupt
Breaks the user habit of fast scrolling with an unexpected opening line.
"Stop scrolling. Before you buy any camera gear, watch this."

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about HookLab, hook writing tips, and how to get more watch time on your videos.

What is HookLab?

HookLab is a free tool that helps you write strong opening lines for short videos. You can use it for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. It stops people from swiping away in the first three seconds. You also get captions, tags, and sound ideas ready to use in seconds.

Why do the first 3 seconds matter so much?

Short video apps watch how long people stay. If viewers swipe away fast, the app stops showing your clip. A good hook grabs their eyes right away and keeps them watching. This boosts your watch time and helps your video reach many more people on their feeds.

How do you write a viral video hook?

Start with a question, a bold thought, or a quick story. Do not waste time saying hello. Jump straight into the main point to make people curious. When viewers want to know what happens next, they stay until the end of your clip.

Is HookLab free to use?

Yes, HookLab is totally free for everyone. You do not need to sign up or pay anything. You can make as many hooks as you want each day. It runs right in your browser, so your ideas stay private on your own device.

Which video platforms does HookLab support?

HookLab is made for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok videos. The tool shapes your hook lines, word pace, and hashtags to match what works best on each app. You can also pick English or Hinglish to fit your audience.

What hook styles work best for creators?

Curiosity hooks and bold takes bring the fastest results. Story hooks work great for personal clips, while how-to hooks are best for teaching tips. Mixing these styles across your weekly videos keeps your page fresh, stops viewers from getting bored, and helps grow your followers.
